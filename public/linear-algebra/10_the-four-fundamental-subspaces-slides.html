The Four Fundamental Subspaces

Every matrix carries four spaces: what you can reach, what does nothing, what you can measure, and the hidden link between your rules.

r, n − r, r, m − r[A | I] → [R | E]MIT 18.06, Lecture 10
The four

Two you know, two new

SpaceLives inMeaning
$C(A)$ columns$\mathbb{R}^m$reachable targets
$N(A)$$\mathbb{R}^n$do-nothing trades
$C(A^T)$ rows$\mathbb{R}^n$what the rules can measure
$N(A^T)$ left null$\mathbb{R}^m$rule combos that are always 0

$m$ = rules (rows), $n$ = funds (columns).

Dimensions

r, n − r, r, m − r

$$ \dim C(A) = r \qquad \dim N(A) = n - r $$ $$ \dim C(A^T) = r \qquad \dim N(A^T) = m - r $$

Row space and column space have the same dimension: each pivot sits in one row and one column.

One reduction

[A | I] → [R | E]

  • $C(A)$: pivot columns of $A$
  • $N(A)$: special solutions from $R$
  • $C(A^T)$: non-zero rows of $R$
  • $N(A^T)$: rows of $E$ next to $R$'s zero rows

$EA = R$ records every row operation.

Nifty, bonds, balanced

All four, worked

$$ [R \mid E] = \left[\begin{array}{ccc|ccc} 1 & 0 & 0.5 & -1.4 & 0.2 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0.5 & 2.4 & -0.2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 1.4 & -0.2 & 1 \end{array}\right] $$

$C(A)$: Nifty, bonds. $N(A)$: $(-0.5, -0.5, 1)$. $C(A^T)$: rows of $R$. $N(A^T)$: $(1.4, -0.2, 1) \propto (7, -1, 5)$.

The row space

What the rules can measure

Rows of $R$: Nifty content $x_1 + 0.5x_3$ and bond content $x_2 + 0.5x_3$.

  • budget = Nifty content + bond content
  • return = 12 × Nifty content + 7 × bond content
  • exposure = Nifty content

Three rules, only two independent measurements.

The left null space

The hidden link

$y = (7, -1, 5)$ gives 0 for every fund:

Nifty $7 - 12 + 5 = 0$, bonds $7 - 7 + 0 = 0$, balanced $7 - 9.5 + 2.5 = 0$

So for every portfolio

$7B - R + 5E = 0$, i.e. $R = 7B + 5E$: the test for which targets are reachable.

The pairs

n = r + (n − r), m = r + (m − r)

Trade side ($\mathbb{R}^n$): measured (row space) + invisible (null space).

Target side ($\mathbb{R}^m$): reachable (column space) + blocked by hidden links (left null space).

Four funds: $4 = 2 + 2$ and $3 = 2 + 1$.

In markets

A complete market

Futures + 20,000 call: left null $(0, 1, 0)$, "payoff at 20,000 is always 0". That scenario can't be touched.

Add the 19,900 call: $N(A^T) = \{0\}$. Every payoff reachable.

Complete market

Left null space = $\{0\}$: no hidden link, every target reachable.

Recap

Key takeaways

  1. Four spaces: $C(A)$, $N(A)$, $C(A^T)$, $N(A^T)$.
  2. Dimensions $r$, $n - r$, $r$, $m - r$.
  3. One reduction: $[A \mid I] \to [R \mid E]$.
  4. Row space: rows of $R$. Column space: columns of $A$.
  5. Left null space = the hidden link = the target test.
