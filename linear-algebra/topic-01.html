Linear Algebra · Topic 01

Vectors & linear equations

Two ways to look at the same set of equations: the row picture (lines and planes meeting) and the column picture (mixing vectors). In markets: how much of each product you need to build a client's payoff.

The lessons

Lesson 1 · with the Options Replication Lab
The Geometry of Linear Equations
Row picture, column picture, Ax = b, matrix times vector two ways, and when a system has no solution. Lab: pick Nifty expiry levels and products, and watch the lots get solved step by step.
Lesson 2
An Overview of Key Ideas
Mixing vectors, a matrix as a machine, running it backwards with the inverse, when it can't be undone, independence and subspaces. Finance: daily P&L and running totals, and building a put from a bond, a stock and a call.
