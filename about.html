About this series

Most quant material starts at Black–Scholes. This series starts underneath it: the maths every model is built from, one idea at a time, tied to the Indian options market.

Where the material comes from

The Linear Algebra notes follow MIT OpenCourseWare 18.06, Linear Algebra, taught by Prof. Gilbert Strang (free at ocw.mit.edu, licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). The notes here are written in their own words, add market examples and labs, and are shared under the same licence. Not affiliated with or endorsed by MIT.

Please note

Everything here is for learning, not a recommendation to buy or sell anything. Market numbers are illustrative; prices, premiums and lot sizes change over time.
