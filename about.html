Why this series

Most quant content starts at Black–Scholes. This series starts underneath it: the maths that every model is built from, one idea at a time, tied to the Indian options market.

Where the material comes from

The Linear Algebra notes follow MIT OpenCourseWare 18.06, Linear Algebra, taught by Prof. Gilbert Strang. The course is free at ocw.mit.edu and licensed under Creative Commons BY-NC-SA 4.0. The notes here are written in their own words, add market examples and interactive labs, and are shared under the same licence: you may share and adapt them for non-commercial use with credit. This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by MIT.

Please note

Everything here is for learning. Nothing is a recommendation to buy or sell anything. Market examples use illustrative Nifty prices, premiums and lot sizes, which change over time. Spotted a mistake? Tell me on LinkedIn and I will fix it.
